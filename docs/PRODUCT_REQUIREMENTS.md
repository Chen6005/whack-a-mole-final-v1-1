# Product Requirements

## Product

A polished browser-based Whack-a-Mole game.

## Audience

Casual players on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## Success Criteria

The player can open the deployed Vercel URL and immediately play a complete game.

## Required User Experience

- Clear home screen
- Easy start
- Smooth gameplay
- Visible score and timer
- Increasing challenge
- Fun visual feedback
- Result screen after round
- Persistent high score
- Works well on phone and desktop

## Non-Negotiable

No placeholder images.
No incomplete screens.
No broken deployment.
No MVP-only delivery.
